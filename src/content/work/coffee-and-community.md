Good cuts are great. Good company makes the visit even better.

At Barbershop 26, there's coffee, cookies, conversation, and a crew that makes the shop feel like a neighborhood gathering place. Come in for a haircut, catch up with familiar faces, meet someone new, or just enjoy the atmosphere.

We're happy to be part of North Hollywood, and we want the shop to feel like a place where people can connect, relax, and feel at home.

Whether you're a regular or visiting for the first time, there's always a reason to stop by.

Good people. Good coffee. Your neighborhood barbershop.
