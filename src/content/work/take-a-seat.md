Not every visit needs to be in a hurry.

Take a moment, get comfortable, and browse the little library at Barbershop 26. Find a book that catches your eye, settle in, and enjoy a break from the usual rush of the day.

![Barbershop 26 Library](/images/barbershop-26/barbershop-26-noho-library.jpg)

Maybe you're waiting for your turn, keeping a friend company, or just stopping by to say hello. There's room for that here, too.

A neighborhood barbershop is about more than the haircut. It's a place to slow down, share a little space, and feel welcome.

**Come on in. Find a seat. Stay awhile.**
